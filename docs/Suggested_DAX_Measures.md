# Suggested DAX Measures for Project Enhancement

| Measure Name | DA Formula (Conceptual) | Description | Suggestion for Visual Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TDS/LBS Ratio** | `[Total Distress Score] / (1 + [Lifestyle Balance Score])` | Normalizes the overall distress score by factoring in general lifestyle balance. A higher ratio indicates that an individual's baseline habits are insufficient to offset their measured psychological burden. | Plotting this ratio against demographics (e.g., age group) can reveal which populations experience disproportionately high stress relative to their efforts at self-care. |
| **Stress-to-Activity Ratio (SAR)** | `SUM('stress_level') / (1 + SUM('physical_activity'))` | Measures the intensity of emotional strain relative to physical activity level. A higher score suggests that an individual's psychological stress is disproportionately high compared to their healthy behaviors. | Use this measure in a scatter plot: X-axis = Physical Activity, Y-axis = SAR. This helps identify if even moderate physical activity can significantly mitigate severe stress levels. |
| **Compounded Risk Score (CRS)** | `(SUM('stress_level') + SUM('anxiety_level')) * SUM('screen_time_before_sleep')` | Quantifies the compounded risk by multiplying psychological distress indicators (Stress + Anxiety) by pre-sleep screen time. This highlights individuals with both high emotional burden and poor digital hygiene habits. | Use this metric for filtering or segmentation. Identify clusters of users where CRS is above a defined threshold, indicating high priority for intervention/follow-up. |
